1. Executive Summary

This guide covers the Company Login section of hr.volkai.io. It details all pages, functions, and workflows for administrators and employees. The portal streamlines recruitment, automates candidate communication, and integrates AI tools such as Calling and WhatsApp Agents.

2. Functional Overview

  • Company Login: Admin access for HR managers. Add employees, manage jobs, configure AI agents.
  • Employee Login: Restricted access for staff to view data without editing privileges.

3. Login Page Overview

  • Choose “Company Login” or “Employee Login.”
  • Enter Email and Password.
  • Successful login directs users to the dashboard.
