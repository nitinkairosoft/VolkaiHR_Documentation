1. Executive Summary

This document provides a comprehensive guide to the Company Login section of hr.volkai.io. It explains all pages, functions, and workflows designed for company administrators and employees. The portal enables organizations to streamline recruitment, manage job roles, automate candidate communication, and leverage AI-powered tools such as Calling Agents and WhatsApp Agents.

The Company Login portal ensures efficient coordination between HR teams and automated AI systems, allowing organizations to focus on strategic hiring decisions rather than repetitive manual tasks.

2. Functional Overview

  • Company Login (Admin Access): For HR heads and managers. Allows adding employees, creating job listings, and controlling AI agents (Calling and WhatsApp).
  • Employee Login (Limited Access): For recruiters or staff with restricted access — can view data but not add new employees.

Both logins share a similar dashboard layout. Access rights are determined by user role and permissions.

3. Login Page Overview

  • Select “Company Login” from the main login screen.
  • Choose between “Company Login” or “Employee Login.”
  • Enter Email and Password, then click Login.
  • Successful authentication loads the dashboard automatically.

  • Add, edit, or delete employee profiles (Admin only).
  • Filter and search by name, department, or designation.

13. Technical Overview

  • AI Voice Agent for phone-based interviews.
  • AI WhatsApp Agent for messaging.
