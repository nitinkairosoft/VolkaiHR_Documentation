🌐 Introduction to Volkai HR Platform

Volkai HR is a next-generation AI recruitment platform that transforms how candidates and companies connect. It leverages Artificial Intelligence to streamline hiring, automate interviews, and deliver actionable insights for both recruiters and job seekers.

The platform is divided into two main portals:

  • User Portal (Candidate Interface): For individuals appearing in AI-driven interviews, reviewing feedback, and enhancing their career through personalized recommendations.
  • Company Portal (Employer Interface): For organizations to manage recruitment, automate candidate communication, and analyze hiring performance.

Volkai HR empowers users through:

  • 🤖 AI-Powered Interviews – Smart, role-specific assessments that mimic real interview experiences.
  • 📊 Instant Analytics – Real-time scoring and feedback highlighting strengths and improvement areas.
  • 💬 Automated Communication – Integrated AI Calling and WhatsApp Agents for seamless candidate outreach.
  • 💼 Career Tools – Resume builder, AI career advisor, and mock preparation modules.

This documentation focuses on the User Login Portal, providing a detailed walkthrough of every page, feature, and process to help candidates make the most of Volkai HR’s intelligent ecosystem.

2. Functional Overview

The platform provides two distinct login types:

  • User Login: For candidates giving interviews and accessing preparation tools.
  • Company Login: For organizations managing hiring operations.

This documentation focuses exclusively on the User Login experience. The key goals of the User Portal include:

  • Easy access to AI interview simulations.
  • Instant AI-driven feedback.
  • Comprehensive career development tools.

7. Previous Interview Reports

The Previous Interview Reports section displays all completed AI interviews. Each record provides a clear performance snapshot and opens into a detailed scorecard.

  • Summarizes past interviews with date, job title, and overall score.
  • Displays a modern Performance Score card with detailed analytics:
    • Overall – Total performance percentage (e.g., 23%).
    • Skills – Technical strength and job role alignment.
    • Clarity – Response precision and structure.
    • Communication – Confidence and verbal fluency.
    • Detail – Completeness and accuracy of answers.
    • Relevance – Context alignment to the asked question.
  • Includes Candidate Name and Designation.
  • Features a Star Rating System for visual performance cues.
  • Shows a circular progress chart for overall performance.
  • Option to open full AI-generated feedback report with detailed metrics and suggestions.
Figure 6: Previous Interview Reports Page showing the new Performance Score layout with metrics, star ratings, and progress indicator.
